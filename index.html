Welcome Note

Tumaini School is a Christian founded learning Institution with Train Up a Child as our Core.We offer Competency Based Curriculum System from Pre – Primary School to Junior School.

founding question

Our Founding question has been how we can train up tender brains into a state of discovering their full potential and the best content delivery methodology for hands on learning experiences.

level based aproach

With understanding that CBC is Spiral, our school has adopted level based structure. So we have Pre – Primary, Lower Primary, Upper Primary and Junior School. Each level is coordinated by a Level Coordinator who checks set standards for authentic transition of each learner.

Discover our approach

Tumaini School is a trusted learning institution that offers quality education from preschool to secondary level for learners aged 3 to 19 years. We are committed to nurturing well-rounded students through strong teaching methods, a supportive learning environment, and a focus on 21st-century skills such as creativity, critical thinking, and leadership. Our goal is to prepare students to succeed academically and become responsible future leaders and innovators.
